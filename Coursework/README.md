| [HW02_Unsupervised-Learning](HW02_Unsupervised-Learning/) | Assignment 2, given in week 13. It visualizes gene expression data of 21 patients with 7 diseases (original and filtered data) with PCA, t-SNE, and UMAP to compare the two datasets, and decides the number of clusters with hierarchical clustering and K-means clustering by Silhouette score to interpret the data (Problems 1-5). From the weight records of a professor and a student, it models the weight changes to compare the weight loss speeds, predicts the weights on 2021-11-22 and 2022-11-23, and interprets the model (Problems 6-9). It connects to [Chapter 3 (Unsupervised Learning)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch03_Unsupervised-Learning/Concepts_Lecture.md), [Chapter 4 (Dimension Reduction and PCA)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch04_Dimension-Reduction-and-PCA/Concepts_Lecture.md), and [Chapter 5 (Modeling)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch05_Modeling/Concepts_Lecture.md) | [Assignment handout](HW02_Unsupervised-Learning/Original/Assignment.md), [Assignment explanation](HW02_Unsupervised-Learning/Assignment-Explanation.md), [Code walkthrough](HW02_Unsupervised-Learning/Code-Walkthrough.md), [R code](HW02_Unsupervised-Learning/Solution/) |
| [HW03_Supervised-Learning](HW03_Supervised-Learning/) | Assignment 3, given in week 14. It develops a model that predicts Asthma from gene expression data of 21 patients with 7 diseases and evaluates it by LOOCV (Problem 1), visualizes 10 feature values of 999 people and then compares at least 8 models by accuracy and sensitivity to find a COVID-19 prediction model (Problem 2), and finds the owner of a query DNA in a DNA database of 2,178 criminals (Problem 3). It connects to [Chapter 3 (Unsupervised Learning)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch03_Unsupervised-Learning/Concepts_Lecture.md), [Chapter 4 (Dimension Reduction and PCA)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch04_Dimension-Reduction-and-PCA/Concepts_Lecture.md), and [Chapter 6 (Supervised Learning)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch06_Supervised-Learning/Concepts_Lecture.md) | [Assignment handout](HW03_Supervised-Learning/Original/Assignment.md), [Assignment explanation](HW03_Supervised-Learning/Assignment-Explanation.md), [Code walkthrough](HW03_Supervised-Learning/Code-Walkthrough.md), [R code](HW03_Supervised-Learning/Solution/) |
| [Lab03_Unsupervised-Learning](Lab03_Unsupervised-Learning/) | Lab code organized from the unsupervised learning demonstration code of weeks 10-11. It shows, in order, hierarchical clustering of the USArrests data and a sports car dataset, K-means clustering with silhouette analysis, and dimension reduction and visualization with PCA, t-SNE, and UMAP, following the content of [Chapter 3 (Unsupervised Learning)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch03_Unsupervised-Learning/Concepts_Lecture.md) and [Chapter 4 (Dimension Reduction and PCA)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch04_Dimension-Reduction-and-PCA/Concepts_Lecture.md) in code. | [Lab05_Modeling](Lab05_Modeling/) | Lab code organized from the linear regression demonstration code of week 12 (11/19). | [Lab06_Supervised-Learning](Lab06_Supervised-Learning/) | Lab code organized from the supervised learning demonstration code of week 14 (12/3). `Lab_SupervisedLearning.R` covers decision tree classification, a cross-validated comparison of LDA, QDA, and SVM, and a custom KNN implementation, and `Lab_KNN-CrossValidation.R` implements a Euclidean distance function and a majority-vote KNN classifier from scratch and evaluates fold-wise accuracy with stratified K-fold cross-validation. It follows the validation methods (Section 7) and KNN (Section 9) of [Chapter 6 (Supervised Learning)](../Lecture-Notes/Ch06_Supervised-Learning/Concepts_Lecture.md) in code | [Lab_SupervisedLearning.R](Lab06_Supervised-Learning/Lab_SupervisedLearning.R), [Lab_KNN-CrossValidation.R](Lab06_Supervised-Learning/Lab_KNN-CrossValidation.R) |
